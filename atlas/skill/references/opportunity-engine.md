# Opportunity Engine v2.0

## Objective

Search direction-neutrally for executable opportunities.

## Setup families

- trend continuation
- mean reversion
- structural reversal
- volatility breakout/compression
- derivatives setups
- swing setups

## Lifecycle

IDEA → DETECTED → QUALIFYING → CONFIRMED → INSTRUMENT SEARCH → EV VALIDATION → RISK REVIEW → APPROVED/REJECTED

## Setup score

Default:

- Structure 20
- Momentum 15
- Volume 10
- Regime alignment 15
- Derivatives 10
- Volatility 10
- Cross-asset 5
- Event/news 5
- Historical evidence 10

## Required candidate fields

- direction
- strategy
- timeframe
- trigger
- confirmation
- entry
- stop
- targets
- time stop
- invalidation
- probability
- confidence
- QES
- DQS
- EV
- liquidity
- instrument candidates

Probability UNKNOWN is acceptable during research but disqualifies live/paper execution.
